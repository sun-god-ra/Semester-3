Waterfall model

The waterfall model is the oldest. It moves in a straight line from communication to deployment, completing each phase before starting the next, and produces documents along the way. It suits projects with stable and well-understood requirements. Its weaknesses are that real projects are rarely so linear, that the customer must state all the requirements at the start, and that no working version exists until late in the project, so mistakes are found when they are expensive to fix.
