Convoy effect

  • If the order is P2, P3, P1: waiting 0, 3, 6 → average = 3. Much better — convoy effect: short processes stuck behind a long CPU-bound process (low CPU and device utilisation). FCFS is not suitable for time-sharing (nonpreemptive)
