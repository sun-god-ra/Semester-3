Access matrix

  • Access matrix — view protection as a matrix: rows represent domains, columns represent objects; Access(i, j) = the set of operations that a process executing in Domain_i can invoke on Object_j

From Operating Systems › Protection

  • Access matrix: rows = domains, columns = objects, entry = rights.

From Operating Systems Unit V questions › question

Protection is the mechanism that controls the access of processes and users to the resources (files, memory, CPU, devices) defined by the system. It is built on domains of protection and access rights (access matrix), and it enforces policies decided by the owner/administrator.
