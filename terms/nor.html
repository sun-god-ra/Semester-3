Term · DLD Lab, Digital Logic

NOR

  • SR latch (NOR): inputs \(S\) (set), \(R\) (reset). Two cross-coupled NOR gates

From Digital Logic › Latches

  • SR latch (NOR): active-high S,R. S=1,R=0 → Q=1 (set); S=0,R=1 → Q=0 (reset); S=R=0 → hold; S=R=1 → forbidden (both outputs 0, and an unpredictable state when released). NAND latch: active-low \(\overline{S},\overline{R}\); set = (0,1), reset = (1,0), hold = (1,1), forbidden = (0,0)

From DLD Lab › Experiment 7 — Latches and flip-flops: SR latch, SR, JK, D, T and master-slave JK

  • Universal gates: NAND and NOR can build any gate. With NAND: NOT \(=\overline{A\cdot A}\); AND \(=\overline{\overline{AB}}\) (NAND followed by NAND-as-NOT); OR \(=\overline{\overline{A}\cdot\overline{B}}\) (De Morgan). With NOR: NOT \(=\overline{A+A}\); OR \(=\overline{\overline{A+B}}\); AND \(=\overline{\overline{A}+\overline{B}}\)

From DLD Lab › Experiment 1 — Logic gates using ICs; NAND and NOR as universal gates

NOR is universal: NOT: \((A+A)'=A'\). OR: \(((A+B)')'\). AND: \((A'+B')'=AB\).

From Digital Logic Design Unit I questions › question

SR latch with NOR gates: \(Q=(R+Q')'\), \(Q'=(S+Q)'\).
