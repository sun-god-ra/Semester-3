Term · DLD Lab, Digital Logic

FPGA

  • FPGA (Field-Programmable Gate Array): a chip with a grid of configurable logic blocks (CLBs), programmable interconnect and I/O blocks

From Digital Logic › Introduction to FPGA

  • Components: A Spartan-6 FPGA board (for example Digilent Nexys 3 / Atlys with XC6SLX16 / XC6SLX45), Xilinx ISE Design Suite (ISE 14.7 is the last version that supports Spartan-6; Vivado does not), USB-JTAG cable, PC

From DLD Lab › Experiment 12 — Introduction to the Xilinx Spartan-6 FPGA: gates and combinational circuits

FPGA (introduction): an array of configurable logic blocks (each with look-up tables and flip-flops), I/O blocks and programmable interconnect, configured by loading a bitstream into SRAM cells. It is reprogrammable and can implement large sequential and combinational designs.

From Digital Logic Design Unit III questions › question

PROM, PLA, PAL, and the complex devices (CPLD) and FPGA. (PROM: fixed AND, programmable OR; PAL: programmable AND, fixed OR; PLA: both programmable.)
