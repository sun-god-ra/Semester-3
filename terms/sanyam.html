Term · UHV-II

Sanyam

  • Relationship of Sanyam and Health — Sanyam (in 'I') ↔ Health (in Body). Sanyam is the 'I's responsibility, Health is the result at the Body level. Without Sanyam, health is lost

From UHV-II › Harmony of 'I' with the Body — Sanyam and Health

  • Relation to Sanyam: with Sanyam (Self-regulation) I use physical facilities in a limited way → leads to prosperity

From UHV-II › Meaning of Prosperity in Detail

  • Case study on Sanyam and Health: a student who sleeps late, eats junk food, uses the mobile phone for long hours → Body falls sick, 'I' is dull → lack of Sanyam → fix by routine

From UHV-II › Practice Session and Case Studies

  1. Health–Self-regulation (swasthya-sanyam): Sanyam and health of the body, and of society; supports prosperity.

From Universal Human Values II Unit III questions › question

Doer, seer, enjoyer: 'I' decides (do), 'I' sees/understands (see), 'I' feels (enjoy). The body only provides the machinery. A useful way to remember this is a driver and a car: the driver decides, sees the road, enjoys the ride; the car carries out the driving. If the car's cravings (tastes, comfort) start to drive the person, the person has lost control; that is the situation without Sanyam.
