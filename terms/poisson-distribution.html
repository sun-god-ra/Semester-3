Poisson distribution

The Poisson distribution has a special property that is asked in many exams: its mean and variance are both equal to \(\lambda\). If a question gives data where the sample mean and variance are almost equal, Poisson is a reasonable model. The distribution is used for counts of rare events in a fixed window, such as misprints per page or accidents per week.

From Prob & Stats › Poisson Approximation to the Binomial

For a Poisson distribution mean = variance = \(\lambda\). So \(\text{Var}(X)=\lambda=\mathbf{3}\) (standard deviation \(\sqrt3\approx1.732\)).
