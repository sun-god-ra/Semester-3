Term · UHV-II

Natural acceptance

  • Natural acceptance = what I naturally accept from within, without any outside pressure, fear, greed or conditioning; it is constant, universal (same for every human), does not change with time, place or situation, and is a feeling in 'I' (not thought)
    • tested by the question "Do I accept this from within?"
    • not the same as liking / disliking (which are preconditioned, changing, based on senses, fashion, company)

From UHV-II › Self-Exploration

  • Basic aspirations of human beings (from natural acceptance): happiness + prosperity — expressed also as fearlessness, trust, harmony, co-existence at higher levels

From UHV-II › Continuous Happiness and Prosperity — Basic Human Aspirations

  • Exercises: (a) list things you like but know to be wrong (e.g., junk food, late-night phone use) — shows like ≠ natural acceptance; (b) "Do I want to be respected? Do I want to respect others?" → natural acceptance says yes to both; (c) find the difference between what I want to be and what I am

From UHV-II › Practice Session — Natural Acceptance vs Liking–Disliking

  • Natural acceptance (A) vs desire (B) vs thought (C) vs expectation (D) — Check using A–D (see Explain)

From UHV-II › Characteristics and Activities of 'I'; Harmony in 'I'

  • Natural acceptance differs: animals live as per breed (fixed) — no wrong behaviour; humans can act against natural acceptance → conflict → hence education is needed
