Term · Digital Logic

EPROM

  • Types: PROM (one-time), EPROM (UV erasable), EEPROM / Flash (electrical)

From Digital Logic › Programmable Logic Devices (PLD)

The classes differ in how and how often they can be programmed. A mask ROM is fixed at manufacture and is economical only for large volumes. A PROM can be programmed once by the user by burning fuses. An EPROM can be erased by ultraviolet light through a quartz window, but the whole chip is erased at once. EEPROM and flash can be erased electrically; EEPROM works one byte at a time and flash works on blocks, which makes flash faster and denser and the choice for storage in phones and SSDs.

From Digital Logic › ROM — Design and Classification

ROM: non-volatile, internal AND-OR decoder structure; Mask ROM (programmed at the factory), PROM (one-time programmable by fusing), EPROM (erased by UV light), EEPROM (electrically erasable), Flash (block erasable). Used for the boot program and fixed look-up tables.
