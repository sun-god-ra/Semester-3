Term · Digital Logic

Don't care

  • Synchronous BCD (decade) counter, JK flip-flops, sequence 0–9 then 0; states 10–15 don't care:

From Digital Logic › Synchronous Counters

(\(m5\) is a don't care and need not be covered.)

From Digital Logic Design Unit II questions › question

Excess-3 code = BCD + 3. Inputs \(A,B,C,D\) (BCD 0 to 9), outputs \(w,x,y,z\). Inputs 10 to 15 are don't cares.

From Digital Logic Design Unit II questions › question

State assignment: \(S_0=00,S_1=01,S_2=10\) (\(Q_1Q_0\)); state 11 is unused (don't care).

From Digital Logic Design Unit IV questions › question

Sequence \(Q_2Q_1Q_0\): 000 → 001 → 010 → 011 → 100 → 101 → 000. States 110, 111 are unused (don't care).
