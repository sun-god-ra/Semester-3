Confidence interval

  • Interval estimate = range of values + confidence level. Called confidence interval (CI).

From Prob & Stats › Interval Estimation

Confidence interval for \(\sigma_2^2/\sigma_1^2\)

From Prob & Stats › Worked Examples from the Lecture Slides (Dr M. V. Rao)

The maximum error is just half the width of the confidence interval. The sample-size formula comes from solving \(E=z\sigma/\sqrt n\) for \(n\). Because \(n\) sits under a square root, halving the error needs four times the sample. And since you can never take 39.34 observations, round up, not to the nearest whole number.

The one that matters most for the later units is \(\text{Var}(\bar X)=\sigma^2/n\): averaging \(n\) readings shrinks the spread by \(\sqrt n\). Every confidence interval and \(z\)-test in Unit III uses it. The \(n-1\) in the sample variance exists for a reason you can see in Proof 4: measuring deviations from \(\bar X\) instead of the true \(\mu\) makes them a little too small, and \(n-1\) corrects exactly that.
