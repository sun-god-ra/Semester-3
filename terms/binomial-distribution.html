Binomial distribution

The binomial distribution answers the question: "if I repeat the same yes/no experiment \(n\) times independently, how many times will I get a yes?" The four conditions (fixed \(n\), two outcomes, independence, constant \(p\)) matter, because exam questions often include a case where one of them fails, and then binomial is not the right model.
