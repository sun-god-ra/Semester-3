Central limit theorem

  • Central Limit Theorem (CLT): for large \(n\) (\(\ge30\)), \(\bar X\) is approx. normal with mean \(\mu\), sd \(\sigma/\sqrt n\), whatever the population shape.

From Prob & Stats › Point Estimation

Central Limit Theorem. \(Z=\dfrac{\bar X-\mu}{\sigma/\sqrt n}\) has a distribution that approaches the standard normal as \(n\to\infty\), whatever the shape of the population.
