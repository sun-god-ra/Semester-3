Half adder

  • Full adder: \(S=A\oplus B\oplus C_{in}\), \(C_{out}=AB+C_{in}(A\oplus B)\) — built from two half adders and an OR. Full subtractor: \(D=A\oplus B\oplus B_{in}\), \(B_{out}=\overline{A}B+\overline{(A\oplus B)}B_{in}\) — two half subtractors and an OR

  • FA = 2 half adders + OR gate

Write the Boolean expressions of a half adder.

From Digital Logic Design Part A › question

Two half adders: HA1 gives \(S_1=A\oplus B\), \(C_1=AB\); HA2 adds \(S_1\) and \(C_{in}\) to give \(S=S_1\oplus C_{in}\), \(C_2=S_1C_{in}\); \(C_{out}=C_1+C_2\) (one OR gate).
