Term · Software Eng

Prototyping

  • Prototyping: build a quick prototype from the initial requirements, let the customer evaluate it, refine it; throw it away or evolve it.

From Software Engineering Unit I questions › question

  • Spiral model (Barry Boehm): risk-driven, combines prototyping with waterfall; series of evolutionary releases; each loop (spiral) = a framework activity: communication → planning → modeling → construction → deployment. Risk analysis at every loop. Cost and schedule adjusted by feedback. Good for large, high-risk systems. Cons: needs risk-assessment expertise, costly, hard to convince customers

From Software Eng › Prescriptive Process Models

  • Other techniques: interviews, questionnaires, observation (ethnography), document analysis, brainstorming, prototyping, JAD workshops
