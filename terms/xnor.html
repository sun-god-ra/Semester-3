Term · DLD Lab, Digital Logic

XNOR

  • XOR properties: \(x\oplus0=x\), \(x\oplus1=x'\), \(x\oplus x=0\), \(x\oplus x'=1\); commutative & associative. XOR of \(n\) bits = 1 if odd number of 1s (odd-parity function). XNOR = 1 if even number of 1s

From Digital Logic › Other Logic Operations and Logic Gates

  • 2-input: \(x\oplus y=xy'+x'y\); XNOR: \(x\odot y=xy+x'y'=(x\oplus y)'\)

From Digital Logic › Exclusive-OR Functions

  • Gates: AND \(Y=AB\); OR \(Y=A+B\); NOT \(Y=\overline{A}\); NAND \(Y=\overline{AB}\); NOR \(Y=\overline{A+B}\); XOR \(Y=A\oplus B=A\overline{B}+\overline{A}B\) (1 when inputs differ); XNOR \(Y=\overline{A\oplus B}\) (1 when inputs are equal)

From DLD Lab › Experiment 1 — Logic gates using ICs; NAND and NOR as universal gates

  • \((A>B)=A\overline{B}\); \((A=B)=\overline{A\oplus B}=AB+\overline{A}\,\overline{B}\) (XNOR); \((A<B)=\overline{A}B\). Exactly one output is 1 for any input

From DLD Lab › Experiment 4 — Single-bit comparator

Truth table for two inputs (A B → AND, OR, NAND, NOR, XOR, XNOR): 00 → 0,0,1,1,0,1; 01 → 0,1,1,0,1,0; 10 → 0,1,1,0,1,0; 11 → 1,1,0,0,0,1.
