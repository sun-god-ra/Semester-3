Term · DLD Lab, Digital Logic

Ripple counter

  • Mod-\(N\) ripple counter (\(N<2^n\)): use \(n=\lceil\log_2N\rceil\) FFs, detect count \(N\) with a NAND gate and feed to the asynchronous CLEAR of all FFs → resets immediately. Decade (mod-10) counter: NAND of \(Q_3\) and \(Q_1\) (count 1010) → clear. 7490 IC
    • state 1010 appears only for a very short glitch time

From Digital Logic › Asynchronous (Ripple) Counters

  • Disadvantage of ripple counters: the propagation delays add, so at high frequency the count is wrong momentarily (decoding glitches); the maximum clock frequency \(f_{max}=1/(n\cdot t_{pd})\). Advantage: simple hardware

From DLD Lab › Experiment 9 — Asynchronous (ripple) counters: up, down and MOD-N

Drawbacks of ripple counters: propagation delays add up (limits speed), decoding glitches.
