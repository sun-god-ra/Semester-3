Home › Terms A–Z › Flip-flop

Term · DLD Lab, Digital Logic

Flip-flop

  • Output often has flip-flop and feedback → can build sequential circuits (registered PAL)

From Digital Logic › Programmable Logic Devices (PLD)

  • State equations (flip-flop input equations): \(A(t+1)=\ldots\) in terms of present state and inputs

From Digital Logic › State Table, State Diagram, State Equations

  • Binary counter with T flip-flops: a \(n\)-bit up counter: \(T_0=1\), \(T_1=Q_0\), \(T_2=Q_0Q_1\), \(T_3=Q_0Q_1Q_2\) (toggle when all lower bits are 1)

From Digital Logic › Counter Design

  • Simplest register: \(n\) D flip-flops, common clock. Parallel load: \(Q\leftarrow D\) on every clock edge

From Digital Logic › Registers

  • D flip-flop (delay): \(Q\) follows \(D\) at the active edge — 7474. T flip-flop: JK with J=K=T; T=1 toggles, so \(f_Q = f_{clk}/2\) (divide-by-2). Preset/clear are asynchronous (act without the clock)
