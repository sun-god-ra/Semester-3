Multilevel feedback queue

  • Multilevel queue and multilevel feedback queue.

From Operating Systems Unit II questions › question

Multilevel queue scheduling splits the ready queue into queues for foreground and background and so on, each with its own algorithm, and a process stays in its queue. Multilevel feedback queue allows a process to move between the queues: a process that uses too much CPU time moves down, and one that waits too long moves up. In the example, Q0 has RR with a quantum of 8, Q1 has RR with 16 and Q2 has FCFS.

From Operating Systems › Scheduling Algorithms

Advantages: (1) different process types get an algorithm that suits them (response time for interactive, throughput for batch); (2) low scheduling overhead, because a process is permanently assigned to its queue; (3) simple, with clear policy for priority between classes. Limitation: starvation of low queues, inflexible (no movement between queues; fixed by the multilevel feedback queue).
