Term · DLD Lab, Digital Logic

Minterm

  • Minterm \(m_i\): AND term containing all variables, each in true or complemented form; equals 1 for exactly one input row. \(x'yz=m_3\) (011)

From Digital Logic › Canonical and Standard Forms

  1. Build the prime implicant chart (rows = PIs, columns = minterms; don't-cares are not columns)

From Digital Logic › Tabulation (Quine–McCluskey) Method

  • Internal: \(k\)-to-\(2^k\) decoder (minterms) + OR gates (one per output bit) with programmable links. Size \(2^k\times n\)

From Digital Logic › ROM — Design and Classification

  • Decoder: \(n\) inputs → up to \(2^n\) outputs; each output is a minterm; with an enable: \(Y_i=E\cdot m_i\). 2×4: \(Y_0=\overline{A_1}\,\overline{A_0}\), \(Y_1=\overline{A_1}A_0\), \(Y_2=A_1\overline{A_0}\), \(Y_3=A_1A_0\). 74138 has active-low outputs and three enables (\(G_1\) high, \(\overline{G_{2A}}\), \(\overline{G_{2B}}\) low)

From DLD Lab › Experiment 5 — Decoders and encoders

  • Essential PI (EPI): a PI that covers a minterm no other PI covers. Must be in the answer

From Digital Logic › The Map Method — Karnaugh Map (K-map)
