Jk flip-flop

Example: counter with sequence \(0\to1\to2\to4\to6\to0\) (binary 000,001,010,100,110), state \(ABC\), using JK flip-flops. Unused: 3, 5, 7 (don't-cares)

From DLD Lab › Experiment 7 — Latches and flip-flops: SR latch, SR, JK, D, T and master-slave JK

The excitation table is the characteristic table read backwards. Given the present and desired next state, it tells what to put on the inputs. For \(0\to0\) on a JK flip-flop, \(J\) must be 0 but \(K\) can be anything, since \(K=1\) also leaves a 0 at 0. That is where the don't-cares come from, and they reduce the logic in a design.
