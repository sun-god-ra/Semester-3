Self-exploration

  • Process of the course = self-exploration, not preaching. Teacher is a co-explorer; we verify every proposal on our own right (nothing to be accepted blindly, nothing to be rejected blindly)

From UHV-II › Purpose and Motivation of the Course

  • Process of self-exploration (how)
    • uses natural acceptance (inner voice) to check every proposal
    • then experiential validation (live it, see if it gives happiness/harmony in actual living)
    • the two together = mutual verification

From UHV-II › Self-Exploration

Self-exploration is the process of finding out what is right for me by investigating my own inner reality (desires, thoughts, expectations) and the outside reality (relationships, nature). It is a process of verification: nothing is accepted or rejected blindly; every proposal is checked on my own right.
