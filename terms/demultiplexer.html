Demultiplexer

  • A decoder with enable can act as a demultiplexer

From Digital Logic › Decoders

  • Difference between a decoder and a demultiplexer?

From DLD Lab › Experiment 6 — Multiplexers and demultiplexers

A demultiplexer sends one input to one of several outputs, chosen by the select lines. It is the same circuit as a decoder when the data input takes the role of the enable: the decoder activates one output line, and when the data is 1 the line is on, and when the data is 0 it is off. This is why decoder ICs are often labelled "decoder/demultiplexer".
