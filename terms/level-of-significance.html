Level of significance

  • \(\alpha\) = level of significance (usually 0.05 or 0.01) = P(Type I)

From Prob & Stats › Tests of Hypotheses

  1. Choose the level of significance \(\alpha\) (usually 5% or 1%).

From Probability and Statistics Unit III questions › question

  • Type I error: rejecting \(H_0\) when it is true. Probability \(=\alpha\) (level of significance).

From Prob & Stats › Worked Examples from the Lecture Slides (Dr M. V. Rao)

  • \(Z=\frac{x-np_0}{\sqrt{np_0(1-p_0)}}=\frac{48-42}{\sqrt{12.6}}=\mathbf{1.69}>1.645\): reject. (Slide wording slip: "0.95 level of significance" should be "0.05".)

From Prob & Stats › Worked Examples from the Lecture Slides (Dr M. V. Rao)

The critical (rejection) region is the set of values of the test statistic for which \(H_0\) is rejected. Its size equals the level of significance \(\alpha\) (probability of a Type I error). Example: for a two-tailed \(z\) test at 5%, \(|z|>1.96\).
