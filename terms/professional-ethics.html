Professional ethics

  • Competence in professional ethics has three abilities (as in the syllabus)
    1. Ability to utilise the professional competence for augmenting the universal human order — use my engineering skill to fulfil the four goals, not only for profit
    2. Ability to identify the scope and characteristics of people-friendly and eco-friendly production systems — systems that serve people and respect nature
    3. Ability to identify and develop appropriate technologies and management patterns for the above production systems — right technologies and right management
