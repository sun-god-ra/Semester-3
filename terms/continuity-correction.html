Continuity correction

  • Binomial is discrete, normal is continuous → use continuity correction (±0.5)

From Prob & Stats › Normal Approximation to the Binomial

  • Outages are whole numbers, so "at least 8" starts at \(7.5\) (continuity correction): \(z=\frac{7.5-11.6}{3.3}=-1.24\)

From Prob & Stats › Worked Examples from the Lecture Slides (Dr M. V. Rao)

  • Large \(n\) (>25 or so): normal approximation with continuity correction
