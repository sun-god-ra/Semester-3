Term · DLD Lab, Digital Logic

Full adder

  • Full adder: \(S=A\oplus B\oplus C_{in}\), \(C_{out}=AB+C_{in}(A\oplus B)\) — built from two half adders and an OR. Full subtractor: \(D=A\oplus B\oplus B_{in}\), \(B_{out}=\overline{A}B+\overline{(A\oplus B)}B_{in}\) — two half subtractors and an OR

  • Adds two \(n\)-bit numbers at once using \(n\) full adders in a chain. Carry out of stage \(i\) → carry in of stage \(i+1\)
