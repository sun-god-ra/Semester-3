Variance

  • Variance \(\sigma^2=E(X^2)-\mu^2\), where \(E(X^2)=\sum x^2 f(x)\)

From Prob & Stats › Random Variables

  • Variance \(\sigma^2=npq\)

From Prob & Stats › Binomial Distribution

  • Mean = Variance = \(\lambda\) (imp. property)

From Prob & Stats › Poisson Approximation to the Binomial

  • Variance \(\sigma^2=\displaystyle\int x^2f(x)\,dx-\mu^2\)

From Prob & Stats › Continuous Random Variables
