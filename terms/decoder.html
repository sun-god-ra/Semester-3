Home › Terms A–Z › Decoder

Term · DLD Lab, Digital Logic

Decoder

  • Implementing Boolean functions with a decoder: OR (or NAND for active-low) the required minterm outputs
    • Full adder: \(S=\sum m(1,2,4,7)\), \(C=\sum m(3,5,6,7)\) → one 3-to-8 decoder + two 4-input OR gates

From Digital Logic › Decoders

  • Encoder: reverse of decoder. \(2^n\) (or fewer) inputs → \(n\)-bit code. Only one input is 1 at a time

From Digital Logic › Encoders

  • Generated by: (1) ring counter; (2) \(n\)-bit counter + \(n\)-to-\(2^n\) decoder (fewer FFs, but decoder glitches); (3) Johnson counter + AND gates

From Digital Logic › Timing Sequences

  • Internal: \(k\)-to-\(2^k\) decoder (minterms) + OR gates (one per output bit) with programmable links. Size \(2^k\times n\)

From Digital Logic › ROM — Design and Classification

  • Increase number of words: stack chips, upper address bits → decoder → chip selects (four \(1\text{K}\times8\) → \(4\text{K}\times8\); address \(A_{11}A_{10}\) select chip)
