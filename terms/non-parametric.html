Non-parametric

  • Non-parametric / distribution-free tests assume little or nothing about the shape. They use signs, ranks, runs, counts

From Prob & Stats › Introduction to Non-Parametric Tests

  • Non-parametric version of one-way ANOVA. \(H_0\): all \(k\) populations identical

From Prob & Stats › Rank-Sum Tests

Explain non-parametric tests.

From Probability and Statistics Part A › question

Used to test whether two independent samples come from identical populations (non-parametric alternative to the two-sample \(t\) test).

From Probability and Statistics Unit V questions › question

Non-parametric alternative to one-way ANOVA for \(k\ge3\) independent samples. Rank all \(n\) observations together, find the rank sum \(R_i\) of each group.
