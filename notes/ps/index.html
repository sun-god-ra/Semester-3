24MA203

Probability and Statistics

Basic Science · 4 credits · L-T-P 4-0-0

Questions and answers

Units

  1. Unit I Probability Distributions and Random Variables6 topics

    Random Variables, Binomial distribution, Hypergeometric Distribution, Poisson approximation to the Binomial distribution, Poisson process.

  2. Unit II Probability Densities and Joint Distributions7 topics

    Continuous random variables, Normal distribution, Normal approximation to the Binomial distribution. Joint Distributions: discrete and continuous.

  3. Unit III Statistical Inference11 topics

    Inferences concerning a mean: point estimation, interval estimation, tests of hypotheses, null hypotheses, hypotheses concerning one mean, relation between tests and confidence intervals, two independent large samples, two independent small samples.

  4. Unit IV Variances and Proportions9 topics

    Inferences concerning variances: estimation, one variance, two variances. Inferences concerning proportions: estimation, one proportion, several proportions, r x c tables.

  5. Unit V Non-Parametric Statistical Tests7 topics

    Introduction, the sign test, rank-sum tests, correlation based on ranks, tests of randomness.
