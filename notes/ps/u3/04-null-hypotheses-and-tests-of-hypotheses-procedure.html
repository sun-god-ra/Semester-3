Probability and Statistics (24MA203)
Unit
Unit III: Statistical Inference
Topic
4 of 11

Practice: Part B questions for Unit III and Part A short questions.

Notebook notes
  • Critical values for \(z\):
\(\alpha\) one-tailed two-tailed
0.10 1.28 1.645
0.05 1.645 1.96
0.01 2.33 2.575
  • Two-tailed: reject if \(|z|>z_{\alpha/2}\). Right-tailed: reject if \(z>z_\alpha\). Left-tailed: reject if \(z<-z_\alpha\).
  • Direction of \(H_1\) decides the tail. "differs / changed" → two-tailed. "more than / less than" → one-tailed.
Explanation

The flowchart is a recipe that works for every test in this unit, and in the later units as well. Steps 1 and 2 are decided before looking at the data. The direction of \(H_1\) decides whether the rejection region is in both tails or only one. Words such as "has changed" or "is different" suggest two tails, and words such as "is greater than" or "has decreased" suggest one tail.

Always finish with a sentence in plain language, for example "there is sufficient evidence at the 5% level that the mean has changed". Marks are usually given for the conclusion in context.
