Digital Logic Design Lab
Lab
  • Experiment 1 — Logic gates using ICs; NAND and NOR as universal gates
  • Experiment 2 — Half adder and half subtractor (and full adder / full subtractor)
  • Experiment 3 — Code converters: Binary ↔ Gray, BCD ↔ Excess-3
  • Experiment 4 — Single-bit comparator
  • Experiment 5 — Decoders and encoders
  • Experiment 6 — Multiplexers and demultiplexers
  • Experiment 7 — Latches and flip-flops: SR latch, SR, JK, D, T and master-slave JK
  • Experiment 8 — Synchronous counters: up, down, up/down and MOD-N
  • Experiment 9 — Asynchronous (ripple) counters: up, down and MOD-N
  • Experiment 10 — Ring counter and Johnson counter
  • Experiment 11 — Shift registers: SISO, SIPO, PISO and PIPO
  • Experiment 12 — Introduction to the Xilinx Spartan-6 FPGA: gates and combinational circuits
