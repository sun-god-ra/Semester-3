Basic Requirements for Fulfilment of Aspirations

Universal Human Values II · Unit I: Introduction, Need, and Basic Guidelines

Basic Requirements for Fulfilment of Aspirations
Subject
Universal Human Values II (24UC201)
Unit
Unit I: Introduction, Need, and Basic Guidelines
Topic
4 of 7

Practice: Part B questions for Unit I and Part A short questions.

Notebook notes
  • Three requirements (and their correct priority):
    1. Right Understanding (understanding) — first priority: clarity about what is true about myself, others, nature, existence. Relationship and physical facility can be fulfilled only with it
    2. Relationship (harmony with other human beings) — second: living in trust and respect with all (family, friends, society)
    3. Physical Facility (basic needs: food, clothing, shelter, medicines, tools) — third: needed, but with a limit (a quantity)
  • Right understanding and relationship give continuous happiness. Physical facility gives temporary happiness (it is needed for the body, not for 'I')
  • Common mistake today: all efforts are for the physical facility (third) and neglect of first two → imbalance, conflict, crisis
Requirement Nature For Priority Quantity
Right understanding qualitative, continuous 'I' (the Self) 1 no limit (always can grow)
Relationship qualitative 'I' 2 no limit
Physical facility quantitative, temporary Body 3 limited (needs a limit)
Explanation

The order Right Understanding → Relationship → Physical Facility is a priority order, not a statement that physical facility is unimportant. Physical facility is necessary (we need food and a shelter), but not sufficient for happiness. The usual exam answer is a table of the three with what they give (continuous vs temporary happiness) and priority. Today people put the third first, so they work hard to get facilities but remain unhappy and also damage relationships.

Note the statement: right understanding and relationship are needed for the Self ('I'); physical facility is needed for the Body. This sets up Unit II.
