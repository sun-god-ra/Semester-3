Basis for Humanistic Education, Constitution and Universal Human Order

Universal Human Values II · Unit V: Implications on Professional Ethics

Basis for Humanistic Education, Constitution and Universal Human Order
Subject
Universal Human Values II (24UC201)
Unit
Unit V: Implications on Professional Ethics
Topic
3 of 6
Terms
Natural acceptance

Practice: Part B questions for Unit V and Part A short questions.

Notebook notes
  • Humanistic education = education that gives both (a) right understanding (values: self, family, society, nature) and (b) skills (profession, technology) — to make a responsible human being (not only a skilled worker)
    • Today: skill-focused, no value education → imbalance
    • Basis: natural acceptance, right understanding (what is to be understood) → then the skill
  • Humanistic constitution = a framework (constitution) of society that is based on universal values and ensures the five dimensions (education–right living, health–self-regulation, justice–preservation, production–work, exchange–storage) for all — and the four goals (resolution, prosperity, fearlessness (trust), co-existence)
  • Humanistic universal order = the order of the world as one family: from the family to the world family; characterised by undivided society, universal order
  • Therefore: humanistic education → humanistic constitution → universal human order (a chain: understanding → system → order)
Explanation

Use the chain education → constitution → universal order. Humanistic education gives both values and skills; a constitution based on human values ensures the five dimensions for everyone; the result is a world family. In the exam, give one sentence for each and a diagram if possible.
