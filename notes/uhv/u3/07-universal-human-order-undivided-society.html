Universal Human Order — Undivided Society

Universal Human Values II · Unit III: Harmony in the Family and Society

Universal Human Order — Undivided Society
Subject
Universal Human Values II (24UC201)
Unit
Unit III: Harmony in the Family and Society
Topic
7 of 8
Terms
Sanyam

Practice: Part B questions for Unit III and Part A short questions.

Notebook notes
  • Undivided society (Akhand Samaj) = a society in which all human beings are related by trust and respect, with no divisions on the basis of caste, creed, race, nation, class, gender ("a world family")
  • Universal Human Order (Sarvabhaum Vyavastha) = an order of all humans living in harmony (undivided society) at every level from family to world; the ten-level extension: family → (10 families) → village → ... → world family
  • Five dimensions of human order (to realise the order in society; each dimension must be fulfilled)
Dimension Meaning (notebook) Value / goal addressed
Education – Right living (shiksha-sanskar) right understanding to all resolution
Health – Self-regulation (swasthya-sanyam) Sanyam and health at all levels prosperity (health of the body, society)
Justice – Preservation (nyay-suraksha) justice in relationships; safety fearlessness (trust)
Production – Work (utpadan-karya) production of physical facility prosperity
Exchange – Storage (vinimay-kosh) fair exchange, storage of resources mutual fulfilment, co-existence
  • From family to world family: family order (mutual trust, respect; justice, prosperity) → extended family → village → cluster → region → nation → world family (the whole humanity as one family)
  • Visualising a universal harmonious order — everyone has the right understanding, a feeling of family for all, everyone has enough (prosperity), all are fearless, and nature is protected (co-existence)
Explanation

Society is just the family extended; if trust and respect work at the family level they work at larger levels. The four comprehensive goals — Resolution (right understanding), Prosperity, Fearlessness (trust), Co-existence — are the aim of an ideal society. The five dimensions are the mechanisms. A neat way to remember the 5 dimensions: Education, Health, Justice, Production, Exchange (each paired with right living, self-regulation, preservation, work, storage).

Undivided society means no walls on the basis of caste, religion, nation. Universal Human Order is this undivided society at every level, from the family to the world family. The syllabus line "Universal Order – from family to world family" is the chain of extension: family → extended family → village/neighbourhood → region → nation → world. The number of steps in the chain differs between sources (some give a ten-level ladder); I could not check the exact count in your prescribed book, so give the idea (extension of the family order), and the count only if your teacher does.
