Understanding the Human Being

Universal Human Values II · Unit II: Harmony in the Human Being: Harmony in Myself

Understanding the Human Being
Subject
Universal Human Values II (24UC201)
Unit
Unit II: Harmony in the Human Being: Harmony in Myself
Topic
1 of 7

Practice: Part B questions for Unit II and Part A short questions.

Notebook notes
  • Human being = co-existence of the sentient 'I' and the material 'Body'
    • 'I' (Self / Jeevan / consciousness) = sentient, the conscious part: the one who desires, thinks, understands, feels (the doer, seer, enjoyer)
    • Body = material, made of the four physical elements (physico-chemical); has no feeling; works as an instrument of 'I'
  • The two are different in nature but co-exist (the body is alive only while the 'I' is in it)
  • Common mistake: "I am the body" (body = I) — we treat the Body as the whole human being, so all our efforts go to the body's needs only
'I' (Self, sentient) Body (material)
Nature conscious, sentient (chetana) physico-chemical, material (jada)
Activities desire, thought, expectation / selection (imagining, analysing, understanding), feeling eating, breathing, moving, digestion, growth, all physical activities
Needs happiness (right understanding, relationship) physical facility (food, clothing, shelter, medicines)
Nature of needs qualitative (feelings) quantitative (can be measured, limited)
Fulfilment continuous, by right understanding and right feeling temporary, by physical facility (consumption stops need)
Knowledge of it cannot be seen (known by being understood in oneself) seen, measured
Perceives its own realisation and understanding senses perceive it
Controls Body (it is the doer, seer, enjoyer) controlled by 'I'
  • Verification: e.g. anger, joy, hope, fear cannot be located in the body; the body does not eat when it is dead (or when the 'I' is absent, as in coma); my thoughts can be dishonest while the body is healthy → two different realities
  • Needs of the Self ('I') and Body
    • Self ('I'): happiness → needs right understanding, right feelings (relationships); fulfilled by understanding; continuous (no limit)
    • Body: physical facility (e.g. food, clothing, shelter, tools, medicines); fulfilled by production; quantity is limited (e.g. you cannot eat endlessly)
    • Common problem: we try to fulfil the needs of 'I' (happiness) with physical facility — like feeding a hungry person with music → fails
Explanation

The key proposal is that you are not just a body. You are a conscious 'I' that uses the body. To verify, notice that what you feel (happy, angry, hopeful) is different from what the body does (digest, breathe). The body needs food, the 'I' needs respect and trust. If you give the body food when the 'I' is lonely (people eat when they are upset), the need of the 'I' is not met; this is a wrong match of need and facility, and it is why we stay unhappy though we have more things.

For the exam: draw the table of 'I' vs Body (nature, needs, activities, fulfilment, quantity). The most important lines are: needs of 'I' are qualitative and continuous; needs of Body are quantitative and temporary (limited).
