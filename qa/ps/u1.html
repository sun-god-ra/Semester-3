Probability Distributions and Random Variables

Probability and Statistics · 3 questions · Notes for this unit

Q1Nov 20258MCO1 K2

A random variable \(X\) has \(P(X=x)=kx\) for \(x=1,2,\dots,8\) (that is \(k,2k,\dots,8k\)). Determine (i) \(k\), (ii) the mean, (iii) \(P(X>2)\), (iv) \(P(2<X<5)\).

Answer

(i) Total probability 1: \(k(1+2+\dots+8)=36k=1\Rightarrow k=\dfrac1{36}\).

(ii) Mean \(=\sum xP(x)=\dfrac1{36}\sum x^2=\dfrac{204}{36}=\dfrac{17}3\approx\mathbf{5.667}\), since \(1+4+9+16+25+36+49+64=204\).

(iii) \(P(X>2)=1-P(1)-P(2)=1-\dfrac{3}{36}=\dfrac{33}{36}=\dfrac{11}{12}=0.9167\). (If the paper means \(P(X\ge2)\) then \(=1-\frac1{36}=\frac{35}{36}=0.9722\). The scanned symbol is unclear, so state which one you used.)

(iv) \(P(2<X<5)=P(3)+P(4)=\dfrac{3+4}{36}=\dfrac7{36}=0.1944\). (If it means \(2\le X\le5\) then \(\frac{2+3+4+5}{36}=\frac{14}{36}=0.3889\).)

Q2Nov 20258MCO1 K2

Two dice are thrown 5 times. Determine the probability of getting a sum of 7 (i) at least once, (ii) two times.

Answer

One throw: sum 7 occurs in 6 of 36 outcomes, so \(p=\dfrac16\), \(q=\dfrac56\). Number of successes in 5 throws is \(B(5,\tfrac16)\).

(i) \(P(X\ge1)=1-P(X=0)=1-\left(\dfrac56\right)^5=1-0.4019=\mathbf{0.5981}\).

(ii) \(P(X=2)=\binom52\left(\dfrac16\right)^2\left(\dfrac56\right)^3=10\cdot\dfrac1{36}\cdot\dfrac{125}{216}=\dfrac{1250}{7776}=\mathbf{0.1608}\).

Q3Expected8MCO1 K3

Three machines A, B, C produce 50%, 30% and 20% of the items, with 3%, 4% and 5% defective. An item is chosen at random and found defective. Find the probability that it came from A.

Answer

Total probability of a defective item: \(P(D)=0.5(0.03)+0.3(0.04)+0.2(0.05)=0.015+0.012+0.010=0.037\). Bayes: \(P(A\mid D)=\dfrac{0.015}{0.037}=\mathbf{0.4054}\). (B: \(0.3243\), C: \(0.2703\); they add up to 1.)
