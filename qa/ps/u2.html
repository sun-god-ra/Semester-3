Probability Densities and Joint Distributions

Probability and Statistics · 7 questions · Notes for this unit

Q1Nov 20258MCO1 K3

A bank receives on average 6 bad checks per day. Find the probability that it will receive (i) four bad checks on any given day, (ii) ten bad checks over any two consecutive days.

Answer

Poisson: \(P(X=x)=\dfrac{e^{-\lambda}\lambda^x}{x!}\).

(i) \(\lambda=6\): \(P(X=4)=\dfrac{e^{-6}6^4}{4!}=\dfrac{0.002479\times1296}{24}=\mathbf{0.1339}\).

(ii) Two days: \(\lambda=12\): \(P(X=10)=\dfrac{e^{-12}12^{10}}{10!}=\mathbf{0.1048}\).

Q2Nov 20258MCO1 K3

Out of 800 families with 5 children each, how many would you expect to have (i) 3 boys, (ii) 5 girls, (iii) either 2 or 3 boys, (iv) at least one boy? Assume equal probabilities for boys and girls.

Answer

\(X\) = number of boys \(\sim B(5,\tfrac12)\), so \(P(X=x)=\binom5x\dfrac1{32}\).

Case Probability Families (×800)
(i) 3 boys \(10/32\) 250
(ii) 5 girls = 0 boys \(1/32\) 25
(iii) 2 or 3 boys \((10+10)/32=20/32\) 500
(iv) at least one boy \(1-1/32=31/32\) 775
Q3Nov 20258MCO2 K2

For the continuous probability function \(f(x)=kx^2e^{-x}\), \(x>0\), find (i) \(k\), (ii) the mean, (iii) the variance.

Answer

Use \(\displaystyle\int_0^\infty x^ne^{-x}dx=n!\)

(i) \(\int_0^\infty kx^2e^{-x}dx=k\cdot2!=2k=1\Rightarrow k=\dfrac12\).

(ii) Mean \(=\int_0^\infty x\cdot\dfrac12x^2e^{-x}dx=\dfrac12\cdot3!=\mathbf3\).

(iii) \(E(X^2)=\dfrac12\int_0^\infty x^4e^{-x}dx=\dfrac12\cdot4!=12\), so Variance \(=12-3^2=\mathbf3\). (This is a Gamma distribution with shape 3, scale 1: mean = variance = 3.)

Q4Nov 20258MCO2 K3

In a normal distribution 31% of the items are under 45 and 8% are over 64. Find the mean and variance.

Answer

Let \(X\sim N(\mu,\sigma^2)\). \(P(X<45)=0.31\Rightarrow z_1=-0.496\) (area 0.19 to the left of the mean). \(P(X>64)=0.08\Rightarrow z_2=+1.405\) (area 0.42 right of the mean).

\[\frac{45-\mu}\sigma=-0.496,\qquad\frac{64-\mu}\sigma=1.405\] Subtract: \(\dfrac{19}\sigma=1.901\Rightarrow\sigma=9.995\approx\mathbf{10}\). Then \(\mu=45+0.496(9.995)=49.96\approx\mathbf{50}\).

Mean \(\approx50\), variance \(=\sigma^2\approx\mathbf{100}\) (exactly 99.9 with the table values).

Q5Nov 20258MCO2 K2

(OR) If \(X\) has the density \(f(x)=2e^{-2x}\) for \(x>0\) (0 otherwise), find the probability that it will take a value (i) between 1 and 3, (ii) greater than 0.5.

Answer

This is exponential with \(\lambda=2\), \(P(X>a)=e^{-2a}\).

(i) \(P(1<X<3)=\int_1^32e^{-2x}dx=e^{-2}-e^{-6}=0.13534-0.00248=\mathbf{0.1329}\).

(ii) \(P(X>0.5)=e^{-1}=\mathbf{0.3679}\).

Q6Nov 20258MCO2 K3

(OR) Two scanners are needed for an experiment. Of the 5 available, 2 have electronic defects, another one has a defect in the memory and 2 are in good working order. Two units are selected at random. (i) Find the joint probability distribution of \(X\) = number with electronic defects and \(Y\) = number with a defect in memory. (ii) Find the probability of 0 or 1 total defects among the two selected. (iii) Find the marginal distribution of \(X\).

Answer

There are \(\binom52=10\) equally likely selections. Types: E (2 units), M (1 unit), G (2 units).

(i) \(f(x,y)=\dfrac{\binom2x\binom1y\binom2{2-x-y}}{10}\)

\(f(x,y)\) \(y=0\) \(y=1\) \(P(X=x)\)
\(x=0\) \(\binom22/10=0.1\) \(\binom11\binom21/10=0.2\) 0.3
\(x=1\) \(\binom21\binom21/10=0.4\) \(\binom21\binom11/10=0.2\) 0.6
\(x=2\) \(1/10=0.1\) \(0\) 0.1
\(P(Y=y)\) 0.6 0.4 1

(ii) 0 or 1 defects: \(f(0,0)+f(1,0)+f(0,1)=0.1+0.4+0.2=\mathbf{0.7}\).

(iii) Marginal of \(X\): \(P(X=0)=0.3,\ P(X=1)=0.6,\ P(X=2)=0.1\).

Q7Expected8MCO2 K2

The heights of students are normally distributed with mean 68 cm and standard deviation 3 cm. Find the probability that a student's height is between 65 and 71 cm.

Answer

\(z_1=\dfrac{65-68}3=-1\), \(z_2=\dfrac{71-68}3=+1\). \(P(-1<Z<1)=2(0.3413)=\mathbf{0.6827}\) (the 68% rule).
