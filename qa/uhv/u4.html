Part B · Unit IV

Harmony in Nature and Existence

Universal Human Values II · 4 questions · Notes for this unit

Q1Apr 20268MCO4 K2

Explain the reasons for the human order not being mutually fulfilling for any of the other three orders.

Answer

Nature's other three orders (material, bio, animal) are mutually fulfilling with each other and with the human order by their natural characteristics: they are self-regulated and they enrich one another. The human order, however, is not mutually fulfilling to them. Reasons:

  1. Wrong understanding: we see nature as a source of resources to exploit; "I am the body" thinking and the wrong notion of prosperity (accumulation).
  2. Living in contradiction with natural acceptance: humans have freedom to choose and often act against what they know to be right (greed, comparison).
  3. Technology without values: used for exploitation, not for mutual fulfilment: deforestation, mining, pollution, plastics, nuclear waste that nature cannot recycle.
  4. Disregard of recyclability and self-regulation, which creates imbalance: global warming, species loss, resource depletion.
  5. Lack of knowledge of the humans' own role: to understand and protect/enrich the other orders. Solution: right understanding, then using technology in line with the cycles of nature (eco-friendly production).
Q2Apr 20268MCO4 K2

Comment on "Existence is co-existence of mutually interacting units". Present the units and orders of existence in the form of a flow chart.

Answer

Existence = everything that is. It has two aspects: space (all-pervasive, accommodates all) and units (in space). Units are not isolated: each is in relationship with others, mutually interacting, each fulfilling and being fulfilled by others (atoms in a molecule, cells in a body, organs in a human, humans in a society, planets in the solar system). Every unit has existence (is) and co-existence (is in space with others). So existence is co-existence of units. Harmony = recognising this and living mutual fulfilment.

Q3Apr 20268MCO4 K2

(OR) Explain natural characteristics, activities and innateness existing in the four orders.

Answer

(⚠ "Innateness" is used differently in some faculty notes; confirm the table below with your notes.)

Order Natural characteristic (svabhav) Activity Innateness / inheritance
Material existence + co-existence (e.g. water flows, fire heats): self-regulation composition–decomposition; recognition and fulfilment structure-based (constituents and bonds decide properties)
Bio existence + life growth, synthesis; seed-to-tree continuity seed-based
Animal existence + life + mind (instinct) desire at the level of body; food, safety, sex breed-based (behaviour fixed by breed, "as per breed")
Human existence + life + mind + knowledge animal-order activities + right understanding, choice, responsibility, imagination sanskar-based (through values, education, understanding)

The first three act in a fixed, harmonious way; the human being has freedom to choose, hence may act against natural acceptance; therefore human beings need education.

Q4Apr 20268MCO4 K4

(OR) Analyse with examples, recyclability and self-regulation in Nature.

Answer
Recyclability Self-regulation
Meaning matter and energy circulate: waste of one is the input for another each unit and order keeps balance in quantity and relationship on its own
Examples water cycle; carbon and nitrogen cycles; dead leaves become compost; cow dung manure feeds crops predator–prey numbers; forests ↔ rain ↔ rivers; population of species; body temperature of the human body
Human disturbance plastic, e-waste, nuclear waste, chemical fertilisers: not recycled deforestation, over-hunting, pollution, loss of vultures or bees leads to cascading imbalance

Together they make nature a harmonious, waste-free and balanced whole. A village pond with its water cycle is a closed loop, a city dump is an open loop. Humans should understand and conform to both when producing and consuming (eco-friendly production).
